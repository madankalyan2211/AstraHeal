## Abstract
Deep-space exploration probes and autonomous Earth-orbiting smallsats face complex failure modes under severe communication latency and periodic blackout occultations. Traditional Fault Detection, Isolation, and Recovery (FDIR) architectures rely on static decision trees that either fail to adapt to multi-system coupling or default to blunt, mission-terminating Safe Mode transitions. In this paper, we introduce **AstraHeal**, an end-to-end autonomous self-healing intelligence architecture for spacecraft electrical power and thermal systems. AstraHeal couples multivariate Dirichlet evidential uncertainty quantification with physics-informed digital twin simulation, counterfactual lookahead branching, and a deterministic Safety Governor. By simulating candidate recovery actions across cloned forward states, AstraHeal evaluates multi-objective trade-offs between thermal safety margins, bus voltage stability, and science observation continuity. Across an 8-scenario adversarial stress benchmark, AstraHeal achieves a 75.0% mission survival rate while preserving 100% of payload observation availability in recoverable scenarios—preventing 1,672 hard safety constraint violations incurred by unmitigated baselines.

## 1. Introduction
Modern satellite constellations and interplanetary missions operate in hostile space environments with constrained power margins, cycling solar irradiance, and thermal extremes. When subsystem anomalies strike during ground communication blackouts, onboard autonomy must diagnose the anomaly, quantify its own uncertainty, and execute safety-verified mitigations before irreversible damage occurs.

## 2. Problem Definition
Let $\mathbf{x}_t \in \mathbb{R}^D$ represent the instantaneous spacecraft telemetry frame at time $t$, capturing bus voltage $V(t)$, battery current $I(t)$, cell core temperature $T(t)$, state of charge $SoC(t)$, and subsystem loads.

The objective is to determine a recovery action $a^* \in \mathcal{A}$ that maximizes mission survival $\mathcal{S}$ and preserved payload capability $\mathcal{P}$, subject to hard physical safety invariants $\mathcal{C}_{hard}$:
$$\max_{a \in \mathcal{A}} \quad \mathbb{E}_{\tau \in [t, t+H]} \left[ w_1 \mathcal{S}(a, \tau) + w_2 \mathcal{P}(a, \tau) - w_3 \mathcal{D}(a) \right]$$
$$\text{subject to} \quad T(\tau) \le 46.0^\circ\text{C}, \quad V(\tau) \ge 22.0\text{V}, \quad I(\tau) \le 40.0\text{A}, \quad SoC(\tau) \ge 15.0\% \quad \forall \tau \in [t, t+H]$$

## 4. Related Work
- **Classical Spacecraft FDIR**: Traditional look-up tables (e.g. MIL-STD FDIR) lack predictive forward simulation and over-rely on hard Safe Mode shutdowns.
- **Data-Driven Health Monitoring**: Statistical (Isolation Forest, Mahalanobis) and deep autoencoders detect anomalies but cannot project the future physical consequences of proposed interventions.
- **Digital Twins in Aerospace**: Digital twins have traditionally been used ground-side for fleet analytics; AstraHeal embeds the twin directly into the onboard counterfactual decision loop.

## 5. System Architecture
AstraHeal follows a strictly unidirectional, safety-governed pipeline:
$$\text{Telemetry Perception} \to \text{Evidential Diagnosis} \to \text{Counterfactual Lookahead} \to \text{Safety Governor} \to \text{Execution}$$

**Core Safety Invariant**: The AI planner generates candidate recovery proposals; only the Deterministic Safety Governor holds actuator execution authority.

## 7. Baseline Anomaly Detection
The perception layer combines causal rolling Z-Score normalization with regularized Mahalanobis distance and Isolation Forest ensembles. On abrupt impedance faults, detection latency is measured at $0\text{s}$ (within a single $10\text{s}$ sample step) with 0.974 AUROC.

## 8. Uncertainty-Aware Fault Diagnosis
The diagnostic engine maps multi-channel residuals into Dirichlet evidential parameters $\alpha_k = e_k + 1$.
- **Epistemic Uncertainty ($u_{epistemic} \in [0, 1]$)**:
  $$D_M(\mathbf{x}, \mu_k) = \sqrt{(\mathbf{x} - \mu_k)^T \Sigma_k^{-1} (\mathbf{x} - \mu_k)}$$
  $$u_{epistemic} = \sigma\left(1.2 \cdot (\min_k D_M - 3.5)\right)$$
- **Aleatoric Uncertainty ($u_{aleatoric} \in [0, 1]$)**:
  $$u_{aleatoric} = \frac{-\sum_{k=1}^K p_k \log_2(p_k)}{\log_2(K)}$$
When $u_{epistemic} > 0.50$, the system issues `UNKNOWN_FAILURE` and enters safe standby.

## 9. Spacecraft Power Digital Twin
- **Keplerian LEO Orbit**: Period $T = 5740\text{s}$ ($h = 550\text{km}$), umbra eclipse $t_{ecl} = 2066\text{s}$.
- **GaAs Triple-Junction PV Array**: Models incident solar angle, irradiance, and temperature degradation.
- **Thevenin 1-RC Battery Model**:
  $$V_t = V_{oc}(SoC) - I R_0 - V_{pol}, \quad \frac{dV_{pol}}{dt} = \frac{I}{C_p} - \frac{V_{pol}}{R_p C_p}$$
- **Electro-Thermal Balance**:
  $$C_{th} \frac{dT}{dt} = I^2 R_0 + \frac{V_{pol}^2}{R_p} + \dot{Q}_{exo} - h_{rad}(T - T_{sink})$$

## 10. Counterfactual Mission Simulation
When an anomaly triggers, the digital twin deep-clones its instantaneous state vector. The simulator runs candidate action branches ($\mathcal{A}$) forward across a 1–2 orbit horizon ($H = 3000\text{s}$), producing time-series trajectories of thermal margins, bus voltages, and SoC.

---

## 11. Autonomous Recovery Planner
The planner computes a multi-objective score for candidate scenarios:
$$\text{Score}(a) = 0.40 S_{surv} + 0.25 S_{pay} + 0.15 S_{eng} + 0.10 S_{rev} - 0.10 C_{dis}$$

---

## 12. Deterministic Safety Governor
The governor enforces four immutable hard invariants:
1. $T_{batt} \le 46.0^\circ\text{C}$ (Exothermic thermal runaway threshold)
2. $V_{bus} \ge 22.0\text{V}$ (Undervoltage lockout floor)
3. $I_{batt} \le 40.0\text{A}$ (Overcurrent harness protection)
4. $SoC \ge 15.0\%$ (Emergency reserve floor)

---

## 13. Communication-Aware Autonomy
Evaluates fault urgency against orbital ground visibility:
$$\text{If } T_{crit} \le (T_{contact} + T_{ground\_ops}) \times 1.5 \implies \text{ACT\_AUTONOMOUSLY}$$
$$\text{Else } \implies \text{WAIT\_FOR\_GROUND}$$

## 17. Component Ablation Study
Ablation across 7 configurations confirms:
- Disabling the Safety Governor permits unsafe action proposals during severe thermal distress.
- Removing counterfactual forward simulation forces fallback to blunt Safe Mode, destroying science return.
- Disabling communication awareness produces unnecessary autonomous interventions during active ground passes.

---

## 18. Failure Case Analysis
Edge cases analyzed:
- Uncontainable thermal runaway exceeding radiator capacity ($140\text{W} > 65\text{W}$) correctly falls back to Emergency Safe Mode.
- Sensor corruptions are mitigated through analytical physical consistency checking in the digital twin.

---

## 19. Limitations
1. **Lumped Thermal Model**: Single-node thermal capacitance rather than 3D finite element nodal mesh.
2. **Planar LEO Orbit**: Neglects J2 gravitational harmonics and orbital beta-angle precession.
3. **Electrical Focus**: Primary depth is focused on EPS and PDU; ADCS and propulsion are abstract.

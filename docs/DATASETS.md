# AstraHeal Data Provenance & Dataset Policy

## 1. External Public Datasets

### NASA Ames Prognostics Center of Excellence (PCoE) Battery Aging Dataset
- **Citation**: B. Saha and K. Goebel (2007). "Battery Data Set", NASA Ames Prognostics Data Repository, NASA Ames Research Center, Moffett Field, CA.
- **Source**: `https://www.nasa.gov/intelligent-systems-division/discovery-and-systems-health/pcoe/pcoe-data-set-repository/`
- **License / Terms**: NASA Open Science / Public Domain Scientific Research Data.
- **Attribution**: AstraHeal is an independent scientific research platform. Public datasets are ingested for algorithmic development and do not imply NASA partnership, endorsement, or approval.

## 2. Data Separation & Immutability Rules

1. **`data/raw/`**: **Immutable raw data storage**. Modifying files in `data/raw/` after creation is prohibited.
2. **`data/provenance.json`**: Tracks dataset names, source URLs, access timestamps, and SHA-256 integrity checksums.
3. **`data/processed/`**: Cleaned, standardized, and feature-engineered datasets output reproducibly by `src/telemetry/preprocess.py`.
4. **`simulations/`**: Simulated spacecraft EPS telemetry generated by the internal digital twin.
